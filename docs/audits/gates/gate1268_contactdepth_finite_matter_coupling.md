# Gate 1268 — ContactDepth FiniteMatter Coupling Canonicality Audit

## Purpose

Audit why matter should become $A_F\otimes Q^3_{contact}$ rather than remaining $A_F$ alone.

$$
A_{depth}=A_F\otimes Q^3_{contact}.
